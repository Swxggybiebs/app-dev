# 🎬 My Favorite Movie: Avengers: Endgame

![Avengers Endgame Poster](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/en/0/0d/Avengers_Endgame_poster.jpg)

## 🦸 About the Movie
**Avengers: Endgame** is a 2019 superhero film produced by **Marvel Studios** and directed by *Anthony and Joe Russo*.  
It serves as the epic conclusion to over a decade of interconnected Marvel stories.

---

## 🌟 Main Characters
- **Iron Man (Tony Stark)**
- **Captain America (Steve Rogers)**
- **Thor**
- **Hulk**
- **Black Widow**
- **Hawkeye**

---

## 💥 Plot Summary
> After the devastating events of *Avengers: Infinity War*, the remaining heroes assemble once more to undo the destruction caused by Thanos and restore balance to the universe.

---

## 🏆 Why I Love It
- Incredible story conclusion  
- Emotional and heroic moments  
- Epic final battle scene  
- The perfect goodbye to some of my favorite characters
